Which type do I pick?

  • Loan / Note / Mortgage — money you borrowed (the 4026 Washington mortgage, an SBA note). JE splits each payment into interest expense + principal, credits cash.
  • Finance lease — equipment you'll keep ($1-buyout, lease runs most of the asset's life). Books an asset you depreciate plus interest. Expense is front-loaded.
  • Operating lease — space or gear you'll hand back. Same asset+liability on the balance sheet, but the P&L shows one flat lease expense every month.
  • Service contract — the plowing guy, the cleaner, your own retainers. No interest — just a scheduled payment and a plain expense (or revenue) entry feeding the cash forecast.
Working tool, v1. ASC 842 lease figures use a standard effective-interest method with monthly periods and initial ROU asset = lease liability (initial direct costs, prepaids, and incentives not yet modeled). This is bookkeeping math to speed your work — not tax or audit advice. Confirm lease classification and account mapping with your treatment before posting.
