The plan

Three fronts, taken one at a time

🔎
Front 1

Your missing refund & the vanished preparer

The refund that was filed but never reached you — possibly routed into the preparer's own account. I get the official record of what actually happened, then go get it back.

  • Pin down the refund amount in question (around $6,753) and where it actually went
  • Pull the IRS's own record — including whether it landed in the preparer's bank, not yours
  • File the formal action to recover it if it was misdirected or taken
  • File a formal misconduct report against the preparer — and escalate as refund theft if that's what it turns out to be
💵
Front 2

The federal child-support share — if there was one

You got the state share. The federal share is unaccounted for — and you can't even see what it should have been. So first we find out if it exists, then where it went.

  • You received the state portion of the offset; the federal portion never reached you
  • Research whether a federal offset amount existed at all — and how much
  • Find out where it went — it may have been diverted, just like the refund
  • Press for answers in writing, so you stop guessing and finally know
🧾
Front 3

Your fiancé's W-2 return

The straightforward one. Regular W-2, federal + Minnesota — done through the Keepstead™ organizer, his choice of two ways.

  • In Keepstead™, pick "W-2 / 1099 Wage Earner" and gather W-2s, ID, last year's return, dependent & bank info
  • Do it himself: he enters it and prints his own reports
  • Or hand it to me: he sends me the info, I complete the organizer, and he prints his reports
