How the money works

Upload the bank statement. It ties itself out.

Money reaches Amy two ways: clients pay her for events, and donors give to You're Not Alone. Both land as deposits in the bank. Amy uploads her monthly statement, every deposit and expense drops into the ledger, she tags each one — which client, which fund, which donor — and it reconciles to the statement's ending balance. The same "tie it out" QuickBooks does, without the QuickBooks learning curve.

Payments in — the business
Client payments become income, matched to the event and invoice
Donations in — the non-profit
Gifts become income, tagged to a fund, donor, and campaign
Reconcile
Upload the statement; it ties to the ending balance and flags what doesn't match
Optional upgrade — automatic bank feeds. Uploading a statement is the starting point. Her bank can connect directly through Plaid — the same rails QuickBooks uses — so transactions sync on their own and she never uploads anything. A paid add-on, added whenever she wants it.
