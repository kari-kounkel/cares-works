Why two subtotals

Minnesota taxes the things Amy hands over — chairs, linens, florals, the arch. It doesn't tax her time — coordinating, managing vendors, walking the rehearsal. Most planners tax the whole invoice and quietly overcharge, or tax none of it and eat the difference at audit. Each line carries its own flag, so the split happens on its own.

Vendors & 1099s · tax year 2026

Amy is the payer here. She hires the florist, the photographer, the DJ — so she's the one who owes them a 1099-NEC. The box does the two things people get wrong: it only counts what she paid by cash or check (card and Venmo-business payments get reported by the processor on a 1099-K, not by her), and it ignores vendors organized as corporations.
