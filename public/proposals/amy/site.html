Ask Amy
Meet Amy

The calm in the middle of your big day.

I've been planning events since 2018, and I simply love it — especially weddings. From my home base in Monticello I turn ballrooms, backyards, and barns into the day your family will talk about for decades. And because I own my décor outright, I can promise you a look and actually deliver it.

My whole job is to lessen your stress. I take one celebration at a time, so on your day you have my full attention — the vendor wrangling, the timeline, and the little fires nobody else even sees me put out.

— Amy

Giving back · You're Not Alone

Every celebration helps put a good dog in a school.

Amy founded You're Not Alone, a non-profit that brings certified therapy teams — one handler, one dog — into schools for student mental health. A share of every event she books helps keep those teams trained, insured, and showing up on the hard days.
