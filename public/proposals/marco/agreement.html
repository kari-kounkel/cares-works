Service Agreement

RCO Certification Review System

Between CARES Consulting Inc and the Minnesota Alliance of Recovery Community Organizations · dated   ↩ proposal

This Service Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between CARES Consulting Inc ("CARES") and the Minnesota Alliance of Recovery Community Organizations ("MARCO"), effective on the date signed below.

1.The work

CARES will design, build, and launch MARCO's RCO Certification Review system and peripherals as described in the proposal at tools.caresmn.com/proposals/marco — including the reviewer console (application, site visit, and panel review), the admin side (reviewer onboarding, availability & assignment, determination letters, and stipends), applicant & reference intake, and a first round of improvements to MARCO's existing website. CARES will deliver the system live within about four weeks of kickoff, with a check-in each week.

2.Fees & payment

Build & peripherals (one-time)$3,500
Support & hosting — one year, starting at signing$1,200
Total due now$4,700

The total is paid in full at signing (by card or invoice). This pricing is locked for the term of this Agreement.

  • Additional modules beyond the scope above are $1,200 per module (flat).
  • Smaller changes or work outside the scope are billed at $120 per hour.
  • All pricing for any additional work is agreed in writing before that work begins. No surprises.

3.Support term

The $1,200 support fee covers one year from the effective date of this Agreement — hosting, updates as the statute and national standards change, and changes to what's built, all directed by MARCO. It renews annually at the same rate unless either party gives notice.

4.What MARCO provides

MARCO will give CARES timely access and the information needed to build (for example, the review forms, sample data, and — for the website work — an Editor or Admin login to its WordPress site), and will review and approve work promptly at the weekly check-ins.

5.Ownership & data

MARCO's data — applications, reviewer records, and all content — belongs to MARCO and is exportable at any time. CARES retains ownership of the underlying software and engine and grants MARCO a license to use it for the term of this Agreement.

6.Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public information confidential and use it only to perform this Agreement. This includes applicant and reviewer data, MARCO's internal materials, and CARES's software, methods, and pricing. Neither party will disclose the other's confidential information without consent, except as required by law. This obligation continues after the Agreement ends.

7.Changes & additional work

Any work beyond the scope in Section 1 is quoted and agreed in writing — at the per-module or hourly rate in Section 2 — before it begins.

8.Term & ending the agreement

This Agreement begins on the effective date and runs through the one-year support term. Either party may end it with written notice. MARCO keeps its data and everything already delivered; fees for work already completed are non-refundable.

9.General

CARES will perform professionally and in good faith. The system is delivered as built; CARES is not liable for indirect or consequential damages, and total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid. This Agreement is the full understanding between the parties, may be changed only in writing, and is governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.
