A quick note

You've built a strong process. Let's make it effortless.

You've often discussed improvements to your review process and documentation. Having had a "front-row seat" to the whole thing, I'm impressed with where you're at — you've done a magnificent job of simplifying the process.

While participating in the most recent review, I thought of some ways to make it even more friendly and comprehensive.

Right now, a Peer Reviewer evaluating an RCO is juggling a JotForm application, a Google Drive full of PDFs, separate forms for the site visit and the panel, and a conflict-of-interest step that lives somewhere else entirely. It works — but it's a lot of tabs, and it's hard to keep a clean, auditable record for a process that runs under a DHS contract — and for a busy team at MARCO.

I built a working preview of something streamlined: a single reviewer console where the applicant's answers, the statute, the uploaded documents, and the scoring all sit on one screen — and the conflict-of-interest recusal is handled before anyone sees a thing. It ends with the close-out: stipend, mileage, and expenses, plus a compilation to use in the response to the applicant.

I recognize there are parts of your process the reviewers (me!) never see, so this proposal is necessarily limited. The actual product is your entire review process, built end-to-end — plus anything else you're currently running through JotForm, Google, or other tools. I'd like to offer you one comprehensive system.

This proposal walks through what it does, how we'd build it for real, and a couple of ways to pay for it so you can pick what fits your budget.

The whole certification pipeline

The reviewer console is one step of seven. Today this process runs on a stack of JotForms plus manual tracking. The full tool absorbs all of it into one system — so the JotForm subscription goes away.

1

Self-assessment & application JotForm ×2 today

The applicant completes the self-assessment, then the full application with ~22 document uploads.

2

Reference survey JotForm today

Three outside references receive and complete a survey about the organization's community work.

3

Preliminary completeness review manual today

MARCO staff confirm the application is complete and request anything missing — handled in the admin panel.

4

Cohort assignment & conflict check

A 3–5 reviewer cohort is assigned; each signs the conflict-of-interest/release, and recused reviewers lose access automatically.

5

Written, site visit & panel reviews JotForm ×3 today

Each reviewer completes the written application feedback, the site visit review, and the panel feedback — the console you previewed.

6

Determination, letter & reporting

MARCO composes the determination letter to the organization — pulling in and editing the reviewers' comments — then the outcome flows to the applicant and to DHS/MHCP, with the record retained for three years.

7

Reviewer stipends & reimbursement JotForm today

Reviewers are paid a stipend per session, plus mileage at $0.725/mile and any expenses — totaled into one invoice automatically and tracked for MARCO's records.

Seven JotForms become one system. Today the process is spread across seven JotForms plus manual tracking. The full tool covers all of it — eliminating the JotForm cost and giving MARCO one clean, auditable record per applicant.
