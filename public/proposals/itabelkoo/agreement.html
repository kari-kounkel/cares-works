1.The work

CARES will deliver a three-session, on-site staff training series for one participant designated by Client — Fridays, August 7, 14 & 21, 2026, 8:30–10:30 a.m. — starting with email (Gmail, attachments, saving to Drive), then Microsoft Word & Excel foundations; Google Drive, Docs, Sheets & Forms; and organization, scheduling & workplace ownership. Includes weekly homework assignments, a 30-day independent practice plan, and a written progress summary suitable for board review.

Follow the training live: the full session-by-session curriculum — with the homework checklists and the 13-skill before/after check — is online at tools.caresmn.com/proposals/itabelkoo/curriculum.html, so the Executive Director can watch progress as items get checked off week by week.

5.Scheduling & cancellation

Either party may reschedule a session with at least 48 hours' notice; a rescheduled session is completed within two weeks of the original date. If Client cancels the series before the first session, no fee is owed. If Client cancels after the series has begun, the flat rate remains due — materials and preparation are delivered in full from Session 1.

6.Confidentiality

CARES treats the participant's individual performance with discretion. Progress reporting is limited to the skills checklist summary and work products, shared with the Executive Director and, at Client's choice, the board. Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential; this obligation continues after the Agreement ends.

7.Independent contractor

CARES is an independent contractor, not an employee of Client. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, or partnership relationship.

8.General

CARES will perform professionally and in good faith. Total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid. This Agreement is the full understanding between the parties, may be changed only in writing, and is governed by the laws of the State of Minnesota.
