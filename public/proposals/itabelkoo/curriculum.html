Start & finish hereThe 13-skill self-check

Rate each skill twice — in the first ten minutes of Session 1, and again at the end of the 30-day plan. 1 = can't do it yet · 2 = can do it with help · 3 = can do it alone. Thirteen skills, because a baker's dozen is a more generous measure than a plain ten.

Session 1 · Friday, August 7Email First — Gmail, then Word & Excel

Goal: leave able to send professional email with attachments, save what you receive to Drive, and create, save, and find documents and basic spreadsheets.

TimeSegmentWhat happens
8:30–8:40Welcome & skills self-checkSeries roadmap; fill in the "Before" column above. This is investment, not punishment.
8:40–9:10Gmail — send & live in the inboxCompose and send; To / CC and when to use each; reply vs. reply-all; subject lines that say what the email is; finding anything with search; archive vs. delete; keeping the inbox workable.
9:10–9:25Attachments & saving to DriveAttach a file to an email (and check it actually attached); open attachments you receive; save an attachment straight to Google Drive and find it again afterward.
9:25–9:30StretchStand up. Five minutes.
9:30–9:55Word & Excel foundationsNew document, Save vs. Save As, file names that make sense (2026-08-07_MemoTopic); the copy-paste clinic (Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V) drilled until boring; spreadsheet basics — cells, sorting, SUM.
9:55–10:20Practice labSend a real email with a properly named attachment; save one received attachment to Drive; build the Provider Contact List spreadsheet from scratch.
10:20–10:30Wrap-upHomework 1 assigned; confirm next Friday.
In-class exercises: the Attachment Round-Trip (send one, receive one, save it to Drive); the Copy-Paste Relay (five items moved between email, Word, and a spreadsheet using only keyboard shortcuts); the Provider Contact List, sorted and saved with a proper filename.

Session 2 · Friday, August 14Google Drive, Docs, Sheets & Forms

Goal: leave able to organize Drive, create and share Docs and Sheets with the right permissions, and build a working Google Form whose responses land in a spreadsheet automatically.

TimeSegmentWhat happens
8:30–8:45Homework reviewOpen the memo, the Drive folder, and the inventory sheet together. Celebrate what worked; fix one thing live.

Homework 2 — due Friday, August 21

Session 3 · Friday, August 21Organization, Scheduling & Ownership

Goal: leave with one trusted task system instead of forty sticky squares, a ten-minute daily planning routine, and a clear map of what to run with and what to ask about.

TimeSegmentWhat happens
8:30–8:40Homework reviewOpen the site log, the shared Sheet, and the Form responses. Three weeks ago, none of this existed.
8:40–9:05The Post-it AmnestyEvery note on the table. Three piles: Do (real tasks), Reference (file it), Done/Dead (recycle with ceremony). Real tasks move into one Master Task List — a Google Sheet with Task / For Whom / Due / Status.
9:05–9:25Daily & weekly rhythmThe 10-Minute Morning: open the master list, pick today's top three, check the calendar. The Friday Fifteen: weekly review — update statuses, clear finished items, set up next week.
9:25–9:30StretchFive minutes.
9:30–9:50Now / Next / LaterSort the real master list together. Urgent-and-important goes Now; important-not-urgent goes Next with a date; everything else is Later. Practice saying when things will happen instead of hoping.
9:50–10:10The Ownership MapA personal decision guide, three columns: Run With It (just do it) · Do It & Report (handle it, note it in the log) · Ask First (spending, policy, personnel, anything affecting clients or houses). Real examples, her own words, printed for the desk.
10:10–10:20Communication rhythmBatching: collect non-urgent questions in one running note; send as one email during work hours. What counts as a true after-hours emergency — and what waits for morning.
10:20–10:30Graduation wrapWalk the 30-day plan below; schedule the 30-day check-in.

On your own · Aug 24 – Sep 18The 30-Day Independent Plan

Four weeks of solo practice, one focus per week. Each Friday: do the Friday Fifteen and send the Weekly Site Log with a one-line note on how the week's focus went.

Week 1 (Aug 24–28) — Live in the system

Week 2 (Aug 31–Sep 4) — Batch and build

Week 3 (Sep 7–11) — Put a form to work

Week 4 (Sep 14–18) — Run the whole show

What happens with all this: the before/after skills checklist plus the site log, the form, and the master list become the written progress summary for the Executive Director and board. The numbers do the talking.
