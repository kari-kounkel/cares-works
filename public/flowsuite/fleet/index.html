Years in transportation. One persistent question.

Built in the yard,
not the boardroom.

Since2014Thinking toward a more connected operation.

Kari spent years in her family's school transportation business. Routes on giant district maps. Colored pencils. Driver sheets, student lists and stop times. Special trips, payroll, billing, vehicles, training and compliance.

This idea comes from doing that work—and asking the same question since around 2014:

“Why are we entering the same information over and over?”

School transportation is where the story starts. The vision extends to vans, delivery and service fleets, trucking, human-services transportation and equipment operations.

Your operation comes first.

Keep the systems
that earn their keep.

The business shouldn't have to reorganize itself around the software.

The intended approach: understand your operation, configure FlowSuite around it, and connect or replace pieces where that creates value. Accounting, payroll and specialized systems can remain part of the plan.

You shouldn't have to buy every possible capability just because it could exist. Implementation starts with a conversation about the work.
