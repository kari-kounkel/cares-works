Who this is for

This is built for an owner with a deadline that has someone else's name on it. Four doors into the same room:

You're selling the business. The buyer's CPA just asked for three clean years and the LOI has a date on it.
You're closing on a bank or SBA loan. The lender wants financials that agree with the tax returns, and the rate lock doesn't care that your bookkeeper quit.
Your books collapsed. The bookkeeper vanished, the bank feed stopped in March, payroll is on fire, and a tax deadline is breathing on your neck.
You run a nonprofit facing an audit or a grant report. The auditor has a calendar. The funder has a form. Neither one is flexible.

If none of those is you, you probably want the monthly bookkeeping engagement instead, and it costs a great deal less. Ask.

What's not included

Scope walls are how the seven days stay seven days. These are available as add-ons or separate engagements:

  • Amended tax returns, or preparing any tax return
  • Payroll corrections, re-runs, or amended payroll filings
  • Sales tax filings or amnesty work
  • 1099 preparation or corrections
  • Inventory counts or valuation
  • More than one legal entity (multi-entity cleanups are quoted separately)
  • Forensic or fraud investigation
  • Ongoing monthly bookkeeping after delivery (we'd love to; it's a different agreement)
  • Migration from software other than QuickBooks Online (quoted on review)

When the 7 days start

Day 1 is the first business day after we receive everything on this list. Nothing arrives by email; it all comes through the CARES Works portal, where you connect your bank and your QuickBooks directly and the checklist turns green as each piece lands. If the list arrives in pieces, the clock waits for the last piece. That protects you as much as us: a week that starts before we can see your bank is not a week.

  • Admin access to your QuickBooks Online file

  • Payroll summaries by quarter

  • A kickoff on the calendar, on site or on video, with the person who can answer questions
