What’s standard turnaround for union orders?

3–4 business days for most products. Rush turnaround (24–48 hours) available for most flat items on request; palm cards specifically we can often turn same-day for canvassing.

Can we get a standing account for our local?

Yes. Standing accounts with locked annual pricing + net-30 terms are what most of our repeat locals use. Contact us to set one up.

Do you work with campaigns as well as locals?

Yes — union-endorsed campaigns get the same union pricing. We’ve worked with local candidates, ballot initiatives, and issue campaigns. Bring us your list, we’ll help spec the job.

What if we need something not listed on the site?

Ask. If it can be printed with the bug, we’ll do it. Custom sizes, unusual stocks, specialty finishes — get a quote.
