1 Getting in

Open the link. You'll land on “Choose your role.” Pick your role, then pick the organization you're working on. That's it — no password.

2 Split up — who plays what

The magic is that we're all in it at once. For the best test, spread across the seats:

4 Walk the flow

As a Peer Reviewer
  • Application Packet — expand a section: the org's answer, the exact statute, and the documents are all right there. On Legal Structure, click a document's “open here” — it opens inline; the page slides left, nothing hides.
  • Written Review — click through criteria 1–15; each is scored with a comment. Change a rating — the other reviewers see it within seconds under “How the cohort scored this.”
  • Site Visit & Panel — notes and the recommendation, all editable.
As MARCO Staff
  • Dashboard — the pipeline at a glance. Schedule & Links — find a time (send reviewers an availability link), then generate a per-person link that drops each person into their step.
  • Intake & Self-Assessment — “+ Add a new RCO” starts a file; flip to the Self-Assessment tab for the real 13-section intake form with a MARCO-notes box on every section and a "resources to send back" checklist.
  • Applications & First Review — the JotForm-killer: per-section feedback → the applicant responds → editing locks. Preview the real first-review email right from the screen.
  • Board — the certification record: the panel's recommendation, each conflict-free member's vote, the tally, and the outcome (with the PIP or certification next-step).
  • Grievances — file & track a grievance about a certified RCO; each one moves New → Reviewing → Resolved.
  • 🗺 Roadmap (far-right tab) — the whole system at a glance: what's Live, In progress, and Planned. Start here to see everything that's built.
As Board / Applicant
  • Board — the panel's recommendation + a live tally; cast your vote.
  • Applicant — a green Certified or red Not certified badge over the full application.

5 Leave notes as you go — this matters most

📝 Look for “Add a note” in the bottom-right of every screen.

Anything you notice — what works, what's confusing, an idea, an edit to make — click it, put your name, and jot it down. It records who wrote it, which screen, and when. Every note lands in one place: MARCO Staff → Record of Engagement — our shared log for this test (and a preview of how the real engagement record will work).

6 Good to know

  • It's all live. Everything anyone does syncs to everyone else in a few seconds — no refreshing.
  • Names are placeholders. “Reviewer 1/2/3,” “Board Member 1/2/3” — obviously not real people yet.
  • Nothing here is precious. This is a test set; we wipe it clean afterward and load the real records.
