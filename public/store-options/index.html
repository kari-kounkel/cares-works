Store Strategy Mockups · Round 2

For Frank Brown · Pick 1–2 directions

Six ways the new store could work.

Same Minuteman branding, same product catalog, same back-office and admin. What varies is the homepage strategy — how a first-time customer lands on the site and where they go from there. Click any option to walk through it end-to-end. Then tell us which 1–2 directions feel right.

All six use Minuteman orange + green, real products, and the Phase 3 branded-storefront upsell. Differentiation is structural, not aesthetic.
OPTION 01
Category-First
Classic Shopify-style structure

Browse by product type

Homepage is a tall grid of category tiles — Cards & Stationery, Marketing, Signs & Banners, Wedding & Events, Apparel & Promo, Reports & Booklets. Customer self-selects what they're shopping for and drills in. Familiar pattern, low cognitive load.

Familiar Browsing customer Low cognitive load
See it
OPTION 02
Use-Case Driven
"What are you working on?"

Lead with the project, not the product

Homepage asks one question — what are you working on — and routes the customer through pre-built paths: starting a business, planning a wedding, opening a store, running an event. Each path surfaces the exact products that project needs. Higher conversion for buyers who don't know what they need by SKU.

Project-led High conversion First-time buyer
See it
OPTION 03
Quote-First Hybrid
File upload is the hero

Built for the print-shop reality

Most print jobs need custom quotes, not click-and-buy. Hero is a big file-upload + quote form ("Have a file? Get a quote in 24 hours"). Browseable product catalog sits below for the customers who do want to buy direct. Honors the way the shop actually runs.

Real-shop fit Custom jobs Quote-led
See it
OPTION 04
Visual Catalog
Pinterest-dense product grid

Photos lead, filters drive

Dense 4–5 column product grid where photography does the selling. Sticky filter sidebar lets shoppers narrow by type, material, quantity, color. Hover reveals price and quick-add. Best fit for visual products (signs, banners, posters) and customers who shop by browsing.

Photo-led Filter-driven High product density
See it
OPTION 05
Bundles & Packages
Solutions, not SKUs

Sell the outcome, not the part

Homepage stars pre-built solution packs: New Business Pack, Grand Opening Pack, Wedding Suite, Restaurant Launch. Each bundle shows contents + bundle savings + single price. Customer buys a complete solution. Bigger average ticket, simpler decision.

Bigger AOV Outcome-led Simpler decision
See it
OPTION 06
B2B / Reorder-First
Account dashboard front and center

Built for the 60% of revenue that's repeat

Homepage assumes you've ordered before. Tiles for Reorder Past Jobs, My Designs on File, Pending Quotes, Quick Reorder. New customers get a separate path. Honors how a real print shop actually makes money — repeat orders from a small set of accounts.

Repeat business Account-led B2B fit
