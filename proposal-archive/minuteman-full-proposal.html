Bookkeeping, documented properly.

The work has been happening for a while — without a written tier or a clear cap on on-site hours. This agreement fixes both. Two tiers to choose from, plus an hourly rate for anything outside the routine.

Tier 2
Bookkeeping Plus
  • Everything in Tier 1
  • Weekly + monthly financials
  • Business meeting attendance
  • HR monitoring with appointed staff
  • General business administration
  • Up to 15 hours/week on-site, as needed

Plus Tier 3 — Hourly Additional Work

Anything outside the selected tier is billed hourly with a written estimate and approval before work begins. Historically that's been: direct customer/vendor negotiations, financial arrangement discussions, the real estate matter, crisis management, and union meetings — all of which now go through Tier 3 with a paper trail.

Sidebar Note

Tier 2 caps on-site presence at 15 hours per week. The current 15–35 hours/week reality isn't sustainable for either of us — this agreement makes the actual relationship visible and bills it accordingly. Sustained work above the cap rolls into Tier 3 or triggers a written renegotiation.

The boring-but-important terms

  • Invoiced by the 25th of the prior month; payment due on the 1st of the service month.
  • Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. Outstanding work and unpaid fees through the termination date remain due; CARES Consulting delivers final reconciled books and reports through the last paid month.
  • Transaction ceiling: 600/month combined across both entities (overages renegotiate or roll to Tier 3).
  • CARES Consulting, Inc. is a Minnesota C-corporation — no 1099-NEC reporting required (W-9 on request).
  • Liability cap: fees paid in the 90 days preceding any claim.

How storefronts get built — your team's workflow

This is the part that makes the whole service line work: your team doesn't have to "build" a new site for each customer. When you're adding or editing a product in the admin, you check off which customers that product belongs to from a dropdown. The customer's storefront automatically shows up at minutemanuptown.com/shop/their-name — no separate build, no developer needed. Tagging products IS the storefront-building workflow.

Sidebar Note

At $300 setup for Basic, you can comfortably resell at $750 and still come in well under your previous $2,500 price point. Standard at $500 supports a $1,250 resale; Premium at $850 supports a $1,750 resale. Every tier protects your margin while making the offer an obvious yes for the customer.

04 · FlowSuite Pro — Pilot Access

FlowSuite Pro, included.

As our pilot customer, Minuteman gets complimentary access to FlowSuite Pro — the multi-module operations platform we're building for print shops and adjacent businesses. No separate contract, no additional fees, no upcharge to your monthly bookkeeping or website investment.

What you get

  • Access to all FlowSuite Pro modules — ScanFlow (barcode job tracking), PeopleFlow (HR), MoneyFlow (financial dashboard), FlowSuite-Print, and the rest of the platform.
  • Runs on CARES infrastructure at a dedicated subdomain (e.g., minuteman.flowsuite.com).
  • Your data on your own Supabase project — you own the database, you own the records, your data lives on your infrastructure.

What's not included

  • No source code — the app runs on our infrastructure; you don't host it or modify it.
  • No feature updates or custom builds — you get the platform as it exists today.
  • No production support obligation — break-fix on your side is your team's responsibility.
  • No reinstall — single pilot deployment.

How it works

  • We set up the Supabase project for you — you'll need one for the commerce site anyway, so one setup covers both.
  • We add your Supabase to FlowSuite Pro's allowlist. Your team logs in at the pilot subdomain. All authentication and data live in your Supabase.
  • If the pilot relationship ends, we remove your Supabase from the allowlist — your data stays with you, app access ends cleanly. No ugly extraction process, no fight over the data.

How long it stays free

FlowSuite Pro pilot access is free as long as your Supabase usage stays within Supabase's free tier. Once your data volume, bandwidth, or compute needs grow beyond the free tier and require paid Supabase, we'll have a conversation about converting to a paid FlowSuite Pro arrangement. Until then — no charge.

Why this works

You get a working operations platform during the pilot phase, at no cost. We get a real customer running real data through the system — invaluable for shaping the product. The arrangement ends cleanly whenever either side wants it to. Your data stays yours. The platform stays ours. Clean lines.
